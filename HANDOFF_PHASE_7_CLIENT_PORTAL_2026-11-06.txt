Sebelum perubahan:

- Petakan booking/client ownership, identity/session, notification, invoice, payment proof, conversation, calendar availability, data repositories, Worker, Firestore rules, dan test contracts.
- Identifikasi behavior yang harus tetap identik.
- Audit responsive behavior di 320–430 mobile, 768 tablet, dan desktop lebar.
- Tentukan targeted tests per modul.

A. Client overview
- Perjelas summary booking aktif/yang akan datang, status permintaan, pembayaran, dan tindakan berikutnya sesuai data model yang ada.
- Jangan membuat metrics/status yang tidak punya canonical source.
- Loading/empty/error/denied state harus jujur.

B. Bookings and calendar
- Booking history, upcoming, calendar, detail, reschedule/cancellation requests sesuai behavior existing.
- Pertahankan booking ID/code, slot/date/time consistency, lifecycle, request/approval, calendar mirror, pricing snapshot, and availability.
- Jangan membuat double-booking atau menampilkan availability dari data stale.
- Mobile list/card lebih nyaman satu tangan. Calendar/table horizontal behavior harus punya cue, tidak memaksa page overflow.

C. Booking detail and conversation
- Pertahankan message identity, actor role, readByAdmin semantics, timestamp, delivery/error state, send idempotency, and error/retry tanpa duplicate.
- Enter/Shift+Enter behavior sesuai PRD.
- Loading, empty, error, disconnected states harus jelas.
- Sticky mobile composer harus menghormati keyboard/safe area.
- Conversation tidak boleh mengungkap booking/customer lain.

D. Payments and proof
- Pertahankan invoice identity, amount, paid/refund/outstanding math, payment status, proof asset identity, upload and approval flow.
- Jangan menampilkan paid/success sebelum canonical transaction selesai.
- Tidak boleh double-submit/duplicate payment proof.
- Retry dan loading state harus menjaga input/context.
- Tidak ada real payment gateway atau real customer proof untuk QA.

E. Client profile/account
- Pertahankan Firebase UID identity, ownership, profile fields, preferences, and auth session boundaries.
- Jangan ubah account-role mapping atau account provision behavior.

F. Notifications
- Audit client-visible notification source dan ownership. Jangan mengasumsikan push/read/unread semantics.
- Pertahankan destination safety, event identity, read receipt/state jika memang ada di source, dan existing routing.
- Jangan menambahkan OneSignal/push delivery baru tanpa scope/contract eksplisit.
- Hindari cross-user notification leakage.

G. Shared components / design
- Reuse primitives dan tokens yang sudah ada.
- Jangan membuat override CSS baru.
- Pastikan Light/Dark, focus, keyboard, forced colors, reduced motion, empty/loading/error/success, dan viewport compact.
- Tidak perlu animasi ornamental; prioritaskan action feedback dan clarity.
